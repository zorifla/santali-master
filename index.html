1. Introduction

Welcome to Santali Master ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), an educational application developed for candidates preparing for the compulsory Tribal/Regional Santali Language Paper (ᱥᱟᱱᱛᱟᱲᱤ / संथाली भाषा, Paper-II) in Jharkhand Government recruitment examinations, including JSSC CGL, JSSC LDC, JSSC Lady Supervisor, JSSC Excise Constable, JSSC Teacher, and JPSC.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information. By installing or using Santali Master, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many educational platforms, Santali Master does not require any user registration or account creation. We do not ask for, collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), including:

  • Your real name, age, date of birth, or gender
  • Your email address or phone number
  • Your contacts or address book
  • Your precise geographic GPS location
  • Photos, camera, microphone, or external storage files
  • Financial details, credit card, or bank account credentials (the app is completely free to use)

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Santali Master uses an offline-first architecture powered by Android's local SQLite / Room database and SharedPreferences. The following academic data is saved exclusively on your phone:

  • Vocabulary Flashcard Mastery: Status of studied Santali vocabulary words (New, Learning, Mastered) with Ol Chiki script, Devanagari pronunciations, Hindi meanings, and 3-part example sentences using Leitner Spaced Repetition.
  • Quiz & Mock Test Records: Chapter quiz answers, mock test scores, time spent, and performance history.
  • Daily Study Streak: Consecutive days the app was opened for revision.
  • App Preferences: Selected filters, display preferences, and local learning milestones.

This data is stored strictly on your local Android device sandbox and is never uploaded, transmitted, or synchronized to any external server.

5. Push Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) & In-App Updates

Santali Master strives to help aspirants maintain regular study discipline through timely updates:

A. Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

The App utilizes Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), an infrastructure service provided by Google LLC, to deliver optional daily study streak reminders, vocabulary flashcard revision prompts, mock test alerts, and important JSSC / JPSC examination syllabus updates.

  • Pseudonymous Token: To route notifications to your device, a randomized, non-personally identifiable Firebase installation token is generated. This token does not contain personal information (such as your name, email, or phone number) and is used exclusively for transmitting study alerts to the general broadcast topic (daily_aspirants).
  • Explicit Permission (Android 13+): On Android 13 (API 33) and later versions, the App strictly requests explicit runtime permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) before presenting any notification alerts.
  • User Control & Opt-Out: You may disable study notifications at any time directly through your Android device settings (Settings → Apps → Santali Master → Notifications → Turn Off). Disabling notifications stops all broadcast alerts immediately while allowing you to continue studying offline without interruption.

B. Google Play In-App Updates

Santali Master integrates the official Google Play In-App Update library to keep your application up-to-date with new exam questions, grammar notes, bug fixes, and performance improvements.

  • Updates download securely and directly through Google Play Store infrastructure.
  • No personal or device data is collected or transmitted to external servers by this mechanism.

6. Permissions Used by the App

Santali Master requests only standard, non-sensitive Android permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to load and render Google AdMob and Meta mediated ads, route FCM notifications, and check for Play Store app updates.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check internet connectivity so the app can gracefully fallback to offline mode when no connection is available.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13 (API 33) and above to display timely daily study streak reminders, flashcard revision prompts, and exam alerts. This permission is completely optional and can be toggled in system settings anytime.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Standard Google Play permission required on Android 13+ to provide a privacy-compliant Advertising ID for ad serving and frequency capping.

The App does not request sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Location, SMS, Phone State, or External Storage.

7. User Control & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Clear All Learning Data: You can completely erase your local database, quiz records, and streak at any time via Android Settings: Settings > Apps > Santali Master > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Manage Notifications: You can turn off study alert notifications anytime via: Settings > Apps > Santali Master > Notifications > Turn Off.
  • Reset Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling the application automatically and permanently deletes all locally stored data from your device.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

9. Data Security
